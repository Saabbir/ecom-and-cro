## What is Qualitative Research?

**Qualitative Research** collects descriptive feedback to answer *"Why are users failing to convert?"*

While quantitative analytics tells you *that* 40% of users leave the cart page, qualitative research reveals *that they left because shipping costs were hidden until the final step*.

## Qualitative Methods

1. **Session Recordings**: Watching anonymized screen recordings of real shoppers interacting with your store.
2. **On-Site Surveys**: Triggering micro-surveys on exit intent asking *"Is there anything holding you back from ordering today?"*.
3. **Usability Testing**: Moderated testing where users perform tasks out loud.
4. **Customer Review Mining**: Analyzing 1-star and 5-star product reviews for recurring customer objections.

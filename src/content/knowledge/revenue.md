## What is Revenue?

**Revenue** (often called the *"top line"*) is the total monetary value collected from selling goods or services over a specified period.

## Gross Revenue vs. Net Revenue

Financial reporting distinguishes between two primary revenue layers:

1. **Gross Revenue**: Total dollar amount of customer purchases before any deductions:
   $$\text{Gross Revenue} = \sum (\text{Line Item Quantities} \times \text{Unit Prices})$$

2. **Net Revenue**: Revenue remaining after deducting refunds, discounts, and order cancellations:
   $$\text{Net Revenue} = \text{Gross Revenue} - \text{Discounts} - \text{Refunds} - \text{Returns}$$

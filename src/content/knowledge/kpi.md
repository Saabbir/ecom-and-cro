## What is a KPI?

A **KPI (Key Performance Indicator)** is a high-level metric selected by a business to track performance against core commercial objectives.

While a store collects hundreds of raw data points, only a select few qualify as true KPIs.

## Core Ecommerce KPIs

1. **Conversion Rate (CVR)**: Percentage of sessions converting into orders.
2. **Average Order Value (AOV)**: Average dollar amount spent per transaction.
3. **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: Total marketing spend divided by new customers acquired.
4. **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**: Total gross margin generated by a customer over their entire relationship.
5. **Cart Abandonment Rate**: Percentage of carts created that fail to complete checkout.

## What is CRO?

**Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)** is a scientific, research-driven process for increasing the percentage of store visitors who take a desired action (e.g. purchasing a product, subscribing to a newsletter, or adding an item to their cart).

CRO is not about guessing design changes or copying competitors—it relies on **data, qualitative research, and statistical experimentation**.

## The Full CRO Cycle

```
Research & Auditing ➔ Opportunity Identification ➔ Hypothesis Formulation ➔ Test Plan ➔ A/B Experiment ➔ Measurement & Learning
```

1. **Research**: Quantitative analytics reviews, heatmaps, user recordings, customer surveys.
2. **Insight**: Identifying exact friction points where users drop off.
3. **Hypothesis**: Formulating evidence-backed theories for improvement.
4. **Experimentation**: Launching controlled A/B tests to measure impact.
5. **Learning**: Implementing winning variations and documenting insights for the next test cycle.

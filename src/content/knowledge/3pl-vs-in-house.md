## Key Differences

- **3PL Fulfillment** is best for growing brands that want to scale shipping volume across global nodes without investing capital into physical real estate.
- **In-House Fulfillment** is best for early-stage stores or premium luxury brands requiring ultra-custom packaging, hand-written notes, or delicate custom assembly.

## Developer Takeaway

<div class="developer-takeaway">
  <p><strong>Hybrid Strategy Support:</strong> Architect your order routing engine to support hybrid fulfillment—routing standard catalog items to a 3PL while routing custom/personalized items to an in-house facility.</p>

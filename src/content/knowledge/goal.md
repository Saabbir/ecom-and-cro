## What is a Conversion Goal?

A **Conversion Goal** is a measurable action configured in analytics and experimentation platforms to quantify user success.

## Types of Conversion Goals

1. **Destination Goal**: Reaching a specific URL (e.g. `/checkout/thank-you`).
2. **Event Goal**: Triggering a specific interaction (e.g. Clicking "Add to Cart" or "Apply Discount").
3. **Engagement Goal**: Viewing >3 pages or spending >2 minutes on site.

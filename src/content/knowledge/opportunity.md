## What is a CRO Opportunity?

A **CRO Opportunity** is a specific page, funnel step, or UI element identified through audit research as having high potential to increase revenue when optimized.

## Prioritization Frameworks (ICE & PIE)

To decide which opportunities to test first, CRO teams use scoring frameworks:

### ICE Framework
- **Impact**: How much improvement will this change generate?
- **Confidence**: How certain are we based on research data?
- **Ease**: How simple is it for developers to build?

$$\text{ICE Score} = \frac{\text{Impact} + \text{Confidence} + \text{Ease}}{3}$$

## Developer Takeaway

<div class="developer-takeaway">
  <p><strong>Focus on High-Traffic Pages:</strong> Prioritize testing opportunities on high-intent, high-traffic pages (Cart, PDP, Checkout) before optimizing low-traffic pages like terms of service or corporate about pages.</p>
</div>

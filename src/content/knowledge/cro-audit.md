## What is a CRO Audit?

An **Ecommerce CRO Audit** is a structured review evaluating a storefront across four primary pillars:

1. **Heuristic & Usability Review**: Evaluating clarity, friction, distraction, and value proposition.
2. **Analytics & Funnel Review**: Analyzing drop-off rates across devices and traffic sources.
3. **Technical Performance & Web Vitals**: Auditing LCP, INP, CLS, and mobile responsiveness.
4. **Accessibility (WCAG)**: Verifying keyboard navigation, focus states, and contrast.

## Audit Funnel Pillars

- **Homepage**: Clear value proposition, fast search bar, navigation clarity.
- **PLP (Product Listing Page)**: Filtering speed, badge clarity, quick-add performance.
- **PDP (Product Detail Page)**: High-resolution media, prominent ATC button, trust signals, size guides.
- **Cart & Checkout**: Sticky checkout buttons, transparent shipping costs, single-page checkout flow.
